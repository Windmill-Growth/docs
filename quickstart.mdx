Teach traxy who you sell to, review your first qualified leads, and choose how to work with them.

If you received an invitation, follow [Join a workspace](/getting-started/join-a-workspace).

## Before you begin

Have your company website and a description of your target buyers ready. Review the trial or subscription terms in the app before entering payment details.

## Set up your workspace with trax

trax guides you through setup in a conversation, with the current draft beside it. You can edit the draft or tell trax what to change. Returning to an unfinished setup resumes your saved progress.

1. Enter your company website. If you do not have one or traxy cannot read it, choose the description option and explain what you sell and who buys it.
2. Answer how you heard about traxy and how strictly leads should match your criteria.
3. Review **Lead criteria**, including at least one buyer location. Review **Intent signals** and correct the topics, pains, and buying triggers that matter to you.
4. Review the people and companies suggested for tracking. Add relevant links and remove suggestions you do not want to track.
5. Review the available posts and first leads. Tell trax when something is off. Setup can continue when there are no posts to show.
6. Draft a LinkedIn campaign template with trax, or skip that step. Preparing the template does not launch a campaign or send messages.
7. Review the trial terms and complete checkout. Wait for payment confirmation and workspace activation before continuing into the app.

The first lead search helps you evaluate targeting. It does not start your trial by itself. Your tracking selection takes effect when the workspace activates.

## Explore your workspace

Choose **Show me around** for a short product tour, or use [Find your way around](/getting-started/navigation). You can replay the tour later from your profile settings.

## Find and review leads

1. Open **trax** and describe who you want to reach. For example: “Discover new leads who are founders discussing their first sales hires.”
2. Review any proposed discovery action and its capacity information before approving.
3. Open the results in **Leads**. Check the person's role, company, ICP match, and recorded signal.
4. Narrow the audience with filters and [save a view](/leads/saved-views) when you want to use it again.

To review people you already have, ask trax to search your existing leads instead.

You can also [track people, posts, or companies](/discovery/tracking) whose audiences are relevant to your workspace.
