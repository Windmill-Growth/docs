Use this page to move from onboarding to your first review of qualified leads.

## Before you begin

- You need access to a `traxy` workspace.
- You should have your company website or domain ready.
- You should know the people and companies you want to target.
- If you want extra signal right away, have competitor, teammate, investor, or custom LinkedIn profile URLs ready to monitor.

If you were invited into an existing workspace, start with [join a workspace](/getting-started/join-a-workspace).

## Complete setup

1. Create or join your workspace.
   If you create a workspace, you become the **Owner**. If you were invited, you may have a shorter onboarding path.
2. Enter your company website or confirm the company domain.
   `traxy` uses it to understand your business and prepare the first ICP.
3. Review how `traxy` works.
   Onboarding explains how `traxy` reads intent signals, monitors Watchlist profiles, and checks engagement against your ICP.
4. Review the ICP draft.
   Check the people, company, and keyword criteria before you continue. Tighten it if the audience is too broad. Broaden it if it looks too narrow.
5. Add Watchlist profiles if onboarding asks for them.
   Paste LinkedIn profile URLs for competitors, teammates, investors, or custom signal sources. You can skip this step and add profiles later.
6. Review the lead preview when it appears.
   The preview shows sample leads, ICP match, top signal, last engagement, and quick fit feedback.
7. Answer the outbound motion step.
   Tell `traxy` whether you already run outbound and choose the channels you use. Onboarding may suggest integrations for those channels.
8. Choose or confirm billing if prompted.
   The app shows the current trial, plan, credit, and monitored-profile details before you continue.
9. Open **Dashboard**.
   Use it to monitor lead volume, fit quality, and top-performing posts.
10. Open **Agent**.
    Use it to control daily profile-scan budget and review automated lead discovery.
11. Open **Leads**.
    Use it to review the people `traxy` identified, classify them, enrich them, and route them.
12. Open **Post Signals**.
    Use it to search posts, qualify a LinkedIn post URL, and run **Find Leads** on specific posts with engagement.
13. Open **Watchlist** if you want to monitor teammate, competitor, investor, or custom profiles.
14. Use the sidebar lead-discovery actions when you want a quick path.
    You can track a profile, score a LinkedIn URL, or pick from recent activity.
15. Connect an integration if you want API or MCP access, Ordinal audience analytics, Slack alerts, HubSpot sync, webhook-style delivery, HeyReach delivery, or Meta Ads audience exports.

## What happens next

- Your dashboard updates as new activity is processed.
- Your leads list grows as `traxy` finds more people who match your ICP.
- Agent can scan matching profiles from the database within your daily budget.
- Post Signals lets you search posts, qualify post URLs, and find leads from specific post activity.
- Watchlist can add more signal if you monitor important LinkedIn profiles.
- Billing and settings show your current plan, usage, profile seats, and workspace controls.

<Info>
  If you do not see qualified leads yet, start with the [no qualified leads yet](/troubleshooting/no-qualified-leads-yet) troubleshooting guide.
</Info>
