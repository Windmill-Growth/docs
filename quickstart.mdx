Use this page to move from onboarding to your first review of qualified leads.

## Before you begin

- You need access to a `traxy` workspace.
- You should have your personal LinkedIn profile URL ready.
- You should know the people and companies you want to target.
- You should have your company website or LinkedIn company page ready.
- If you want extra signal right away, have one competitor, teammate, investor, or custom profile ready to monitor.

If you were invited into an existing workspace, start with [join a workspace](/getting-started/join-a-workspace).

## Complete setup

1. Create or join your workspace.
   If you create a workspace, you become the **Owner**. If you were invited, you may have a shorter onboarding path.
2. Add your personal LinkedIn profile during onboarding.
   `traxy` verifies your profile before setup can continue.
3. Confirm your company.
   Choose the company `traxy` should use for your workspace. If the right company is missing, add or edit it, then confirm the company domain before you continue.
4. Add a profile to Watchlist if onboarding asks for one.
   You can choose a recommended profile, paste a LinkedIn profile URL, or skip this step and add profiles later.
5. Review the ICP draft `traxy` generates for your workspace.
   Tighten it if the target audience is too broad. Broaden it if it looks too narrow.
6. Let onboarding finish the first qualification pass.
   `traxy` starts matching recent LinkedIn engagement and watched-profile activity against your ICP.
7. Wait for the workspace finalizing step if it appears.
   `traxy` may briefly show a setup screen while it finishes workspace creation in the background.
8. Open **Dashboard**.
   Use it to monitor lead volume, fit quality, and top-performing posts.
9. Open **Leads**.
   Use it to review the people `traxy` identified, classify them, enrich them, and route them.
10. Open **Post Signals**.
   Use it to search posts, qualify a LinkedIn post URL, and run **Find Leads** on specific posts with engagement.
11. Open **Watchlist** if you want to monitor teammate, competitor, or investor profiles.
   You can also use custom relationship labels when the standard profile types do not fit.
12. Use **Find More Leads** from the sidebar when you want quick access to common lead-discovery actions.
   You can add a profile to Watchlist, qualify a post by URL, or qualify a past post.
13. Connect an integration if you want Slack alerts, Webhook delivery, or Meta Ads audience exports.

## What happens next

- Your dashboard updates as new activity is processed.
- Your leads list grows as `traxy` finds more people who match your ICP.
- Post Signals lets you search posts, qualify post URLs, and find leads from specific post activity.
- Watchlist can add more signal if you monitor other important LinkedIn profiles.
- Billing and settings show your current plan, usage, profile seats, and workspace controls.

<Info>
  If you do not see qualified leads yet, start with the [no qualified leads yet](/troubleshooting/no-qualified-leads-yet) troubleshooting guide.
</Info>
