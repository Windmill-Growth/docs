Use onboarding to teach traxy who you sell to, review a real lead preview, and start your workspace.

If someone invited you to an existing workspace, follow [join a workspace](/getting-started/join-a-workspace). Your setup may be shorter.

## Before you begin

Have these ready:

- your company website
- a clear description of the people and companies you want to reach
- LinkedIn profile URLs for useful signal sources, such as competitors, teammates, influencers, investors, clients, or partners
- a payment card for the seven-day Standard Pro Trial

## Complete onboarding

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm your company">
    Enter your company website. traxy uses the public company context to prepare your Signal Brief and ICP.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review how traxy works">
    Learn how the Agent finds leads and how Watchlist turns engagement on selected LinkedIn profiles into qualified leads.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve your Signal Brief">
    Review the people and company criteria in the generated ICP. Edit any field that does not describe your target buyer, then approve it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Watchlist profiles">
    Add LinkedIn profiles whose audiences matter to you. You can skip this step and add profiles later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the lead preview">
    Review sample leads and the signal behind each match. Mark examples as good or bad fits to give traxy early feedback.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the Standard Pro Trial">
    Review the current trial terms in the app and add a card. The Standard Pro Trial lasts seven days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your outbound motion">
    Tell traxy which outbound channels you use. traxy can point you to the relevant integrations.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Review your workspace

After onboarding:

1. Open **Dashboard** to review lead volume, recent trends, audience segments, and top-performing Watchlist profiles and posts.
2. Open **Agent** to review its status, daily credit budget, targeting summary, and run history.
3. Open **Leads** to qualify, enrich, classify, and route people.
4. Open **Post Signals** to find leads from specific LinkedIn posts.
5. Open **Watchlist** to add or manage profiles.
6. Open **Integrations** to connect the API, Claude, Codex, or a delivery destination.

<Info>
  If your first leads have not arrived, work through [no qualified leads yet](/troubleshooting/no-qualified-leads-yet).
</Info>
