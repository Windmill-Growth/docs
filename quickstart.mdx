Teach traxy who you sell to, review your first qualified leads, and choose how to work with them.

If you received an invitation, follow [Join a workspace](/getting-started/join-a-workspace).

## Before you begin

Have your company website and a description of your target buyers ready. Review the trial or subscription terms in the app before entering payment details.

## Set up your workspace

1. Enter your company website and confirm the company context.
2. Review the generated **Lead criteria** and **Signals**. Correct anything that does not describe your buyers or the topics that matter to them.
3. Approve your targeting and review the lead preview. Use fit feedback to explain a mismatch.
4. If the preview is still running or has no results, use the offered option to continue without waiting.
5. Choose the available trial or paid option and complete checkout. Review the confirmation before continuing.

The lead preview helps you evaluate targeting. It does not start your trial by itself.

## Explore your workspace

Choose **Show me around** for a short product tour, or use [Find your way around](/getting-started/navigation). You can replay the tour later from your profile settings.

## Find and review leads

1. Open **trax** and describe who you want to reach. For example: “Discover new leads who are founders discussing their first sales hires.”
2. Review any proposed discovery action and its capacity information before approving.
3. Open the results in **Leads**. Check the person's role, company, ICP match, and recorded signal.
4. Narrow the audience with filters and [save a view](/leads/saved-views) when you want to use it again.

To review people you already have, ask trax to search your existing leads instead.

You can also [track people, posts, or companies](/discovery/tracking) whose audiences are relevant to your workspace.
