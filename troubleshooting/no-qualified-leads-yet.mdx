---
title: "No new qualified leads"
description: "Find out whether discovery is waiting, paused, or finding too few matches."
---

Check the workspace and cause before requesting another run.

## Check the view

Confirm the workspace, lead view, search, score filter, and date filters. A saved view can hide otherwise visible leads. Look in **Done**, **Snooze**, **Clients**, **Bad fit**, or **Deleted leads** when appropriate.

## Check discovery status

Open **trax** > **Discovery runs** and inspect the latest run. A request can be queued, running, finished, or unable to start. A completed run with few matches is different from a run still processing.

## Check capacity and access

Review the pipeline overview. If capacity is reached, new discovery pauses until capacity becomes available. If the workspace is read-only or requires reactivation, restore access through the offered action.

If capacity remains but supply is low, an upgrade is not the first fix. Review targeting and sources.

## Review targeting and sources

Check **Lead criteria** for overly narrow or conflicting requirements. Check **Signals** for relevant topics. Confirm tracked profiles are active and have relevant activity.

Inspect any available candidate or lead results. Use concrete mismatch reasons to adjust targeting instead of lowering a display filter and expecting qualification to change.

## Check the destination separately

If leads appear in traxy but not another tool, discovery worked. Review the [integration route](/integrations/routes), connection, and delivery result instead of restarting discovery.
