## What is the difference between the Agent and Agent runs?

**trax** is the workspace chat assistant. **trax** > **Discovery runs** shows discovery history and controls. Searching your existing leads is separate from starting discovery for new people.

## Does a scanned profile count as a new lead?

No. Pipeline capacity counts a person when first delivered as a visible qualified lead in your workspace. Scans and repeated signals are separate.

## Does a filter change qualification?

No. A filter changes the results you see. Update **Lead criteria** or **Signals** to change discovery targeting.

## Does fit feedback change the score?

Feedback records your verdict and reasons. It does not directly rewrite the score. Review and save targeting changes separately.

## Can I reuse an audience?

Yes. Save a lead view and share it when members, campaigns, or routes need it. The view stores filters, so its matching people can change over time.

## Does a campaign test send messages?

No. Review the test and launch approval separately. A live campaign can still require message approval depending on its delivery mode.

## Why does a message wait after approval?

Sender availability, schedules, limits, prior steps, replies, and suppression can affect delivery. Inspect the lead's status rather than resubmitting the same request.

## Do integrations send existing leads automatically?

Saving or editing a route does not backfill the audience already in the view. Send those leads manually when appropriate.

## Does MCP use the workspace I last selected in the app?

No. Each scoped call uses its explicit `workspaceId` or the connection's original default. See [MCP workspace selection](/api-reference/mcp/workspaces).

## Where do I find billing and refund terms?

Review **Settings** > **Plan** and **Billing**, the terms shown at checkout, and the [refund policy](/refund-policy).
