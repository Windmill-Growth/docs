---
title: "Sender accounts"
description: "Connect LinkedIn accounts and mailboxes for outreach in traxy."
---

Open **Outbound** > **Sender accounts** to connect the accounts your workspace uses for outreach. You can also ask trax to help connect a sender.

## Connect an account

1. Choose **Add sender** and the available account type.
2. Choose the sender name the account belongs to.
3. Complete the connection flow. If sign-in opens in another tab, finish there and return to traxy.
4. Confirm that the account appears as connected.
5. Review **Set up sending**, including the timezone, sending days, and hours. For LinkedIn, review connection requests per day and **Ramp up**. Choose **Save** to keep your settings or **Later** to use the offered defaults.

Accounts are grouped by sender name. A sequence using LinkedIn and email needs a sender name with both connected channels.

For Gmail, enter the address you send from. A work address uses the Google Workspace flow. If your domain has not been connected before, follow the displayed Google admin setup and confirm that your admin has trusted traxy before continuing. Personal Gmail uses an app password through its separate flow.

## Collect LinkedIn activity

A LinkedIn account can collect activity without sending messages. Configure its activity in **Targeting** > **Intent signals** > **LinkedIn activity**. To use the account for outreach, review its sending state and enable sending when appropriate.

Disconnecting a LinkedIn account can stop both activity collection and outreach using that account. Review the affected uses before confirming.

## Review sending controls

Review the stored limits, active hours, and ramp-up controls available for the account. The app shows the values that apply to that sender; a campaign does not bypass them.

For email, review the sender signature. Sequence and one-off emails include it when present. LinkedIn messages do not use the email signature.

## Reconnect or remove

Reconnect an account when its connection needs attention. Confirm the intended workspace and sender name before completing sign-in.

Before removing a sender, review the affected campaigns in the confirmation. It shows which leads move to other senders and which pause until an account is available. A campaign losing its only sender stops taking new leads. Wait for this information to load before confirming.

Removal does not retract delivered messages or delete their history. If an account is disconnected, review the offered reconnect or connection action before expecting activity collection or outreach to resume.
