---
title: "Do not contact"
description: "Suppress outreach to people who should not receive further messages."
---

Use **Settings** > **Do not contact** to review outreach suppression. Choose email or LinkedIn, enter the email address or profile identifier requested by the form, and add the entry.

You can also upload a CSV with `kind,value` columns. Use `EMAIL` for email addresses and `LINKEDIN` for LinkedIn entries. Review the file before uploading it so the type and value match the contact you intend to suppress.

Suppression affects outreach eligibility. Unsubscribe requests and hard bounces also stop active outreach. Check the suppression reason before deciding whether a record should be removed.

## Choose the right action

| Your goal | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Prevent outreach to a person or contact | **Do not contact** |
| Exclude a company's domain from lead discovery | [Blocklist](/workspace/blocklist) |
| Finish work on a lead | **Done** |
| Return to a lead later | **Snooze** |
| Record that someone does not match your ICP | Fit feedback |

Removing a suppression is not approval to resend an earlier message. Review the person's current sequence and conversation before starting new outreach.
