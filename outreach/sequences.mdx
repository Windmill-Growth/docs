---
title: "Sequences and one-off messages"
description: "Prepare LinkedIn and email outreach and review each sending action."
---

Use a sequence for several steps over time. Use a one-off message for a single action to one lead.

## Prepare outreach

Open a lead's **Message** action or ask the Agent to prepare outreach. Review **To**, **From**, the channel, and the message content.

For a sequence, review each step and the wait before it. A wait can depend on an event such as connection acceptance. Choose sender names with the channels the sequence requires.

A lead can be in only one active sequence at a time. Check existing outreach before enrolling the same person elsewhere.

## Approve sending

For one step, **Send** is the approval. Adding follow-ups creates a sequence; **Activate sequence** approves activation and enrollment for the selected lead.

After activation, inspect the recorded status. **Sending**, **Sent**, **Not sent**, and **May have been sent** have different meanings. An uncertain result must not be treated as a failed send and repeated blindly.

## Pause and resume

Pausing a sequence keeps each lead's position and stops further delivery until it resumes. You can also pause a lead's enrollment from its Inbox conversation. Resuming the whole sequence does not release a lead you paused separately.

Replies, unsubscribe requests, and hard bounces stop active outreach. Sender availability, spacing, and limits can also delay the next step.
