Open **Leads** and select **Companies** to review the companies in your current view. **People** and **Companies** use the same audience filters.

## Inspect a company

A company row shows its matching people count, best ICP match, latest signal, industry, size, and available research. Expand the row to review its people, or open the company panel.

The panel includes:

- **Overview** for company information.
- **People** for people in the current view and other available people at that company.
- **Ads** for recorded Meta ads and when they were checked.

Choose **Show their people** for selected companies, or open a company’s people count, to return to **People** with a company filter. Clear that filter when you want the broader audience again.

Use **Track company** to follow its public activity. Manage the relationship later under **Targeting** > **Companies**. A tracked company can appear before it has matching people.

## Check Meta ads and traffic

Use **Check Meta ads** above the table to check the companies across the current view. To check only selected companies, use the selection action. You can also check a company from its **Ads** tab.

Checks start immediately. Review the result after **Checking…** finishes. Recently checked companies may reuse their existing results. A missing result or an unchecked company is different from a company with no active ads.

Filter by **Running Meta ads**, **Active Meta ads**, or **Monthly visits** when these conditions help define your audience. The **People** table can also display Meta ads and monthly visits as optional columns.

In a saved view, **Check new companies** checks previously unchecked companies automatically. It uses the saved view’s filters; save filter changes separately.

These research checks are separate from [exporting a Meta Ads audience](/integrations/meta-ads).
