---
title: "Contact details"
description: "Find contact data, manage enrichment, and review saved details."
---

Open a lead to review saved work email, personal email, phone, and profile information before requesting another lookup.

## Find contact details

Choose the lookup action for email, phone, or both. Email lookup is included with eligible plan access. Phone lookup requires the phone add-on or an entitlement that includes it. It applies to leads delivered after phone enrichment was activated. Turning it on does not unlock historical phone lookups. Review the availability shown for the individual lead.

A lookup is not a guarantee that a usable address or number exists. Check the saved result and its status before using it.

Contact lookup does not consume pipeline capacity or make the person a new qualified lead.

## Automatic enrichment

Use **Auto-enrich** in **Leads**, or **Settings** > **Company** > **Lead enrichment**, to manage automatic email lookup. It starts off and applies to leads delivered after you turn it on. Turning it off and on again starts a new boundary.

Use the separate historical email action to look up email for existing leads. Review whether to include leads marked **Done**. Manual email and export lookups do not require you to enable automatic enrichment.

Saved contact details remain available after you disable automatic email or remove the phone add-on, while workspace access permits reading them.

## Manually supplied details

You can edit a lead’s work email, personal email, or phone in the app, or ask trax to save details you provide. Review the proposed value before approving. Manually supplied details are saved as unverified, and enrichment does not overwrite them. Entering a phone number does not require the phone add-on. Clearing a manual value makes that field eligible for lookup again.

Future messages use the updated contact details. Previously delivered integration records are not automatically sent again.

## Export or send contacts

Review the selected fields and destination before exporting or routing leads. Public API and MCP access to contacts requires the appropriate [contact scope](/api-reference/authentication).
