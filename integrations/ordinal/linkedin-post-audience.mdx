---
title: "Get LinkedIn post audience"
description: "Read aggregate audience analytics for a LinkedIn post tracked in the workspace."
openapi: "api-reference/customer-api-mcp-v1.openapi.yaml GET /audience-analytics/linkedin-posts"
---

Use this endpoint to read aggregate-only audience data for a tracked LinkedIn post.

Supply `postUrn`, or pass the `quoteId` returned by an audience-discovery request. At least one is required. Reading the result does not start discovery or consume pipeline capacity.

When discovery is pending, poll with the returned `quoteId`. Inspect `quote` and `latestRequest` for progress. A `requestId` appears after the prepared quote is accepted. If `quote.replacedByQuoteId` is present, follow that replacement quote.

An unknown quote or a quote from another workspace returns `404 not_found`.
