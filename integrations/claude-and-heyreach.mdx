---
title: "Use Claude with a HeyReach workflow"
description: "Combine traxy data access with a separately authorized HeyReach workflow."
---

Use the traxy connection to review lead evidence in Claude. Use a traxy route or campaign destination to move the selected audience into HeyReach.

1. [Connect Claude](/integrations/claude) to the intended traxy workspace.
2. [Connect HeyReach](/integrations/heyreach) and choose the destination.
3. Ask Claude to review a small audience and explain the evidence behind its selection.
4. Review the selected leads and proposed messages before authorizing delivery or launch.
5. Inspect HeyReach's campaign and sender configuration before outreach begins.

If you also connect HeyReach directly to Claude, follow the connection instructions in your HeyReach account. That authorization is separate from traxy's MCP connection.

For a native traxy campaign, an authorized MCP client can create and update campaigns, test a sample, review the launch preview, and request launch. The available actions depend on your granted scopes and workspace permissions. Follow [campaign review and launch](/campaigns/review-and-launch) and consult the [public tool reference](/api-reference/mcp/tools) before starting outreach.
