title: "Slack"

Connect Slack when you want lead alerts and workspace updates in your team's channels.

## Connect and test

1. Open **Integrations** > **Slack** and complete the Slack authorization.
2. In **Settings**, review the connected workspace and default channel.
3. Invite the traxy app to a private channel if it does not appear in the picker.
4. Review the available triggers, lead-reply notifications, daily summary, and message customization.
5. Save the configuration and use **Test trigger**.

The default channel receives the notifications and tests configured for it. Lead routes can use their own channels.

## Route an audience

Open **Routes** and add the lead view, destination channel, and delivery mode. You can create a channel from the channel picker when that action is available.

Follow [Route leads to other tools](/integrations/routes) for automatic delivery and sending existing leads manually.

## Handle lead replies

In **Settings**, use **Post lead replies** to enable notifications. Choose LinkedIn, email, or both, and use the default channel or select another channel.

When **Reply from Slack** is enabled, the notification includes a reply box and **Send on LinkedIn** or **Send email**. Review the recipient and channel before sending. **View conversation** opens the conversation in traxy.

## Review notifications

### Review campaign messages

For campaigns that require human approval and send through a traxy sequence, configure **Approval notifications** on the campaign. Choose a channel or use the workspace's default. Choose each-message notifications or batched reviews.

An individual notification includes **Approve**, **Rewrite**, **Skip**, and **Open in traxy**. In a batch, choose **Review** for one lead or **Review all in traxy**. Review the saved text before approving. Your Slack email must match a confirmed email for an active member of the traxy workspace.

Turning notifications on posts new approvals from that point; it does not send the existing queue to Slack. Previously posted messages continue to reflect edits and decisions made in Slack or traxy. If a message changed while you were reviewing it, refresh and review the latest version.

### Review lead alerts and summaries

Use **Settings** to configure repeat-engagement events and daily summaries. Repeat engagement concerns new activity from a qualified lead; it is not another newly delivered lead.

If notifications stop, check the connection, trigger, route state, and channel access. Fix the issue before resuming a paused route. A successful test confirms the test request, not all future deliveries.
