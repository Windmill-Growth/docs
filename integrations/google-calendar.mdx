---
title: "Google Calendar"
description: "Connect selected calendars to attribute meetings to traxy leads."
---

Connect Google Calendar so traxy can match calendar meetings to leads in your workspace.

1. Open **Integrations** > **Google Calendar**.
2. Connect the intended Google account and review its authorization request.
3. Select the calendars to use for attribution.
4. Save the selection and allow synchronization to complete.
5. Open **Meetings** to review matched results.

## Manage calendars

Review the selected calendars when you change accounts or team responsibilities. Refresh the calendar list if a calendar is missing. A removed calendar or rejected permission can stop its synchronization.

## Check a missing meeting

Confirm the Google account, selected calendar, date range, and sync status. Then review whether attendee information can match the lead or company. An event on a connected calendar is not automatically an attributed meeting.

Reconnect when authorization expires. Follow the displayed sync error if a calendar is no longer available or Google temporarily refuses access.

See [Meetings and outcomes](/reporting/meetings) for reviewing attribution and recording results.
