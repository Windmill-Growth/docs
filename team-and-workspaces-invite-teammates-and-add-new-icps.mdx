## Add a new workspace

Growing your team and juggling multiple ICPs shouldn't slow you down — here's what to set up:

- Invite unlimited teammates for free from Settings → Profiles & Users, assigning each one as a Member or Admin
- Their invite goes out as a pending email — copy the link, re-invite, or remove access anytime
- Spin up a new workspace whenever you take on a different ICP, either running full onboarding for a new site or skipping straight to setting the ICP and signal brief yourself
- Each new workspace gets its own 7-day trial, and you can switch between workspaces anytime

More hands, more workspaces — same traxy account, zero extra cost.
