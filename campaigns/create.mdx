title: "Create a campaign"
description: "Write a campaign yourself or work with trax to research an audience and prepare outreach."
---

Use a campaign to apply a research or messaging plan to a lead audience.

Campaign actions depend on your workspace's access and plan. If **Campaigns** shows an access or upgrade message, resolve it before trying to launch.

## Write a campaign

1. Open **Outbound** and choose **Create campaign**.
2. Name the campaign.
3. Choose the lead lists under **Audience**. With none selected, the audience is **All leads**. Use **See leads** to inspect it.
4. Write the message steps under **Sequence**, or select an available saved sequence.
5. Under **Senders and delivery**, choose senders with the accounts needed by every step. Check that sending is enabled.
6. Choose whether you approve each message or messages send automatically.
7. Review **Still needed** and resolve any issues before choosing **Launch campaign**.

Check that the draft shows **Saved**. If it changed elsewhere, reload it before continuing. Opening or saving a draft does not launch it.

## Build with trax

Start in **trax** when you want help with research, evidence, or messages written for each lead. Describe your audience, offer, and desired outcome. Identify the shared lead view and explain the research facts or message context you need. Review the plan and answer questions about missing information.

For example: “Use our shared founder view. Research how each company handles outbound, then draft a short LinkedIn introduction based on the evidence.”

## Choose what happens to results

A campaign can use the available integration, HeyReach, or sequence destination. Review the named destination, sender, and delivery mode. Research-only work does not require you to invent an outreach message.

Connecting a sender or destination is separate from authorizing launch. Use [Sender accounts](/outreach/senders) or [Integrations](/integrations/overview) to complete setup.

## Test a campaign built with trax

Ask trax to test the plan and inspect the results. A test prepares research and previews without sending outreach. Continue with [Review and launch](/campaigns/review-and-launch) when the sample meets your requirements.
