---
title: "Review and launch"
description: "Inspect campaign tests and approve the audience, messages, and destination."
---

For a campaign built with trax, review its test results in the campaign panel beside the chat. For a campaign you write in the builder, use **Review and launch** to inspect the audience, sequence, senders, and delivery mode.

## Review the test

Check whether the research supports each claim. Inspect missing or skipped results as well as usable drafts. Ask trax to revise the plan when it relies on the wrong audience, unsupported facts, or unsuitable wording.

A successful test does not mean a message was sent. Review the resulting preview and the campaign's next action.

## Launch the campaign

When the test finishes, open **Launch** and review:

- who will join the campaign
- leads excluded because another unfinished campaign holds them
- people contacted by a completed campaign before
- the selected sender or destination
- message steps and delivery mode

Confirm **Launch** only when these settings are correct. This is the campaign launch approval.

## Review messages that need approval

If the delivery mode requires approval, inspect leads in the campaign’s review queue. Review each message and its scheduled step before choosing **Approve** or **Approve all**.

You can edit a message before approving it. After an edit, choose **Save**, then approve the saved copy. **Cancel** discards the edit. If another member has changed or decided the message, refresh the approvals and review the latest version.

Changing the delivery mode to automatic does not release messages already waiting for approval. Review those messages separately.

Approval allows the next work to proceed; delivery can still wait for sender availability, schedule, limits, or other eligibility conditions. Check each lead's status to confirm the outcome.
