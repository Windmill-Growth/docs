---
title: "Meetings and outcomes"
description: "Review meetings attributed to traxy leads and record their outcomes."
---

Use **Meetings** to review meetings attributed to your leads. Connect [Google Calendar](/integrations/google-calendar) and select the intended calendars first.

## Review attribution

Open a meeting to inspect its matched lead or company. Attribution depends on available attendee and lead information; connecting a calendar does not mean every event will match.

Use the available date and target filters to review a consistent audience. Check calendar selection and sync state if an expected meeting is missing.

## Record an outcome

Review the meeting and choose the outcome that reflects the conversation. **Active opportunity** records ongoing interest without moving the lead to Clients. **Already a client** and **Closed won** represent different outcomes; choose based on the actual relationship.

## Compare results

Use meeting counts alongside discovery and campaign outcomes. A delivered message is not a reply, and a reply is not a booked meeting.

If you use the API, the [API reference](/api-reference/overview) includes aggregate meeting analytics and an attributed meetings list. Contact permissions control attendee detail access.
