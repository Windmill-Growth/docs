Open **Performance** to review your workspace's lead volume, recent trends, and audience patterns.

## Choose a consistent scope

Review the date range and available filters before comparing numbers. Keep the same workspace and scope when you move between Performance, Tracking, and Leads.

## Review results

Use the lead summary and trend chart to understand how discovery is progressing. Review the available audience breakdowns and top profiles or posts to identify useful sources.

A profile scan, a post engagement, and a delivered qualified lead are different events. Several posts can include the same person, so adding post-level counts may not equal a unique lead total.

## Decide what to change

- If matches are weak, inspect individual leads and refine targeting.
- If volume is low, review source activity and discovery status.
- If capacity is reached, review [your plan and capacity](/workspace/plan-and-capacity).
- To review attributed conversations that became meetings, open [Meetings](/reporting/meetings).

Use underlying lead and signal details to explain a result before changing your configuration.
