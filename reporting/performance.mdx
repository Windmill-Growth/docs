Open **Insights** to see how leads arrive, how your team acts on them, and which sources contribute results.

## Choose a consistent scope

Choose **This cycle** or **Last 7 days**. Use **All teammates** for the workspace, or choose a tracked teammate to focus on leads from that person's posts. This filter describes the source of the leads, not the member assigned to them.

Keep the same workspace and period when comparing Insights, Tracking, and Leads. Use **Clear filter** to return to the default scope.

## Review results

| Card | What to review |
| --- | --- |
| **Leads** | With **This cycle** and **All teammates**, review plan usage against capacity when a capacity period is available. Other selections show leads for that scope. |
| **Leads acted on** | The share of leads given a status, manually sent to an integration, enrolled in a campaign sequence, or sent a one-off message. Automatic integration delivery alone does not count as team action. |
| **Meetings booked** | Meetings attributed to your leads. Connect Google Calendar when prompted. |
| **Daily leads** | Daily delivery, split into **Found by trax** and **From Tracking**. Select a day to open its leads. |
| **Messages and replies** | LinkedIn campaign messaging, replies, and reply rate. Its campaign picker filters this card only. |
| **Lead breakdown** | The recorded signal categories for the selected audience. |
| **Top tracked people**, **Top tracked companies**, or **Top tracked posts** | Sources ranked by qualified leads for the selected period. Change the card's view to compare source types. |

The lead breakdown distinguishes **Warm** leads that match your signal brief, **Related** activity on a relevant topic, **Activity** without a matching brief topic, and **ICP fit** without a strong recorded signal.

Plan usage and the number of currently visible leads can differ. **Leads acted on** and the breakdown can include completed or bad-fit leads that are absent from your active lead list.

A profile scan, a post engagement, and a delivered qualified lead are different events. Several posts can include the same person, so adding post-level counts may not equal a unique lead total.

## Refresh incomplete data

If one card fails to load, use its **Retry** action. Other cards can remain available. A load error does not mean zero results. If the page shows an earlier update time, let it refresh or choose **Refresh** before comparing figures.

## Decide what to change

- If matches are weak, inspect individual leads and refine targeting.
- If volume is low, review source activity and discovery status.
- If capacity is reached, review [your plan and capacity](/workspace/plan-and-capacity).
- To review attributed conversations that became meetings, open [Meetings](/reporting/meetings).

Use underlying lead and signal details to explain a result before changing your configuration.
