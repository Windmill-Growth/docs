title: "How qualification works"
description: "Understand fit, signals, feedback, and qualified lead delivery."
---

traxy evaluates people against your lead criteria and records the available signal context. Qualified leads become visible when your workspace can receive them.

## Understand fit

Qualification requires the workspace's criteria and a successful ICP match of at least 50%. Filtering for a higher score narrows your visible results; it does not change the qualification threshold.

Signal strength and ICP match answer different questions. Check both the person's fit and the evidence behind the lead before contacting them.

## Understand discovery sources

- **Agent runs** discover people from your targeting and signals.
- **Tracked profiles** surface people through relevant post activity.
- **Tracked posts** let you request discovery for a particular LinkedIn post.

The same person can have several recorded signals without becoming several new leads.

## Give feedback

Use **Good fit** or **Bad fit** and explain the reason. Feedback records your judgment; it does not directly rewrite the person's score. Review proposed targeting changes separately.

Actions such as **Done**, **Snooze**, and **Client** organize work. They are separate from fit feedback.

## Understand capacity

A person uses pipeline capacity when first delivered as a visible qualified lead in your workspace. Rediscovery, additional signals, export, enrichment, or restoring that lead do not count the person again.

When capacity is reached, new lead discovery pauses. Review [pipeline capacity](/workspace/plan-and-capacity) for what continues and how discovery resumes.
