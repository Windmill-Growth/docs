---
title: "LinkedIn activity"
description: "Find qualified leads from connected profiles and company-page activity."
---

Open **Targeting** > **Intent signals** > **LinkedIn activity** to choose which activity becomes leads for your workspace.

## Connect an account

1. Choose **Add LinkedIn account** and complete the connection flow.
2. Select the activity you want to collect: **Profile views**, **New followers**, and **New connections**.
3. Review the account’s connection status and enabled signals.

You can connect an account for activity collection without enabling outreach. Sending messages requires a sender with sending enabled. See [Sender accounts](/outreach/senders).

## Follow company-page activity

Under **Company pages**, review the pages available through your connected accounts. Turn on **Watch** for the intended page, then choose whether to collect **Followers**.

If a page is missing, check which LinkedIn account manages it. Follow the connection guidance to sign in again when traxy cannot retrieve its pages. Do not disconnect an account without checking the outreach that uses it.

Company-page follower collection is separate from [tracking a company’s public posts](/discovery/tracking).

## Review the resulting leads

In **Leads**, use the **LinkedIn activity** filter to choose the activity, connected account, and period. Activity must still meet your workspace’s qualification and capacity requirements before it becomes a new qualified lead.

A connected account can be collecting before matching leads appear. If it disconnects, earlier collected activity remains visible, but new activity waits for reconnection.
