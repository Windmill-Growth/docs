Open **trax control panel** > **Lead criteria** to describe who you sell to.

## Define your ICP

Use people criteria for roles, departments, seniority, and locations. Use company criteria for size, industry, revenue, funding, and relevant keywords or niches. Review the company and product context so traxy understands your offer.

Start with requirements that distinguish a buyer from a broad audience. For example, “sales leaders at software companies” is more useful than a long list of unrelated titles.

## Save a change

1. Review the existing criteria.
2. Change the fields that no longer describe your target buyer.
3. Review and save the update.
4. Inspect subsequent qualified leads and their fit reasons.

An ICP update can offer a separate review of existing active leads. Review the reported mismatches before confirming that they should move to **Done**. Saving targeting alone does not authorize that move.

## Keep fit and signals separate

Lead criteria describe who fits. [Signals](/discovery/signals) describe the evidence you want to find. A relevant post does not make someone a qualified lead if they do not meet your criteria.

A lead-list score filter narrows the results you see. It does not change the qualification rules or create additional qualified leads.
