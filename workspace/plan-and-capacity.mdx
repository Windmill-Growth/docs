---
title: "Plan and pipeline capacity"
description: "Understand how qualified leads use your workspace capacity."
---

Open **Settings** > **Plan** to review your plan and included features. Check the pipeline overview for usage and the current period.

## What counts

A person uses capacity once, when first delivered as a visible qualified lead in your workspace. Finding that person again, recording another signal, enriching contact details, exporting, deleting, or restoring them does not count them again.

All lead-discovery sources share the same workspace capacity. Candidate and scan counts are not charges for delivered leads.

## When capacity is reached

Discovery of new people pauses. Monitoring, signals, and enrichment for delivered leads can continue while access permits them.

An upgrade, add-on leads, or the next capacity period can resume discovery. Do not expect a backlog of all people encountered at capacity to arrive automatically afterward.

## Review periods and changes

The app shows the period, reset date, and available plan actions. Capacity periods reset monthly for paid plans, including annual billing. Unused capacity does not roll over.

Review the confirmation for when a plan or add-on change takes effect and any amount due today. Upgrades and reductions can have different effective dates.

## Contact data and campaign access

Email lookup, phone enrichment, and campaign availability have their own entitlement rules. Review the features shown for your workspace. Contact enrichment does not use pipeline capacity.

If discovery reports low supply while capacity remains, refine targeting or add useful sources. Buying more capacity does not solve a lack of matching people.
