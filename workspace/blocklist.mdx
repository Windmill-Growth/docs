---
title: "Blocklist"
description: "Exclude companies or people from your workspace's lead results."
---

Use **Settings** > **Blocklist** to exclude companies by domain or people by LinkedIn profile. Choose **Companies** or **People** before adding an entry or uploading a CSV.

## Block a company

1. Add the company domain using the blocklist action.
2. Review the normalized domain.
3. Confirm the exclusion.

You can also start a domain exclusion from a company's hover card in **Leads**. Review the company carefully so you do not exclude an unrelated domain.

## Block a person

Open **People**, choose **Add people**, and enter the person's LinkedIn profile URL. Review the profile before confirming. For a CSV upload, use the format shown for the selected blocklist type.

## Review exclusions

Blocklisted people and people at blocked companies are excluded from normal lead results. They are not moved to deleted leads. Removing an entry lets otherwise eligible leads appear again and allows future discovery to evaluate them. It does not guarantee new leads or replay past discovery.

Lead CSV exports do not use the blocklist as an exclusion rule. Review the export audience separately when you need to exclude these contacts from a file.

Blocklist is different from [Do not contact](/outreach/do-not-contact). Use outreach suppression when the goal is to stop messages to a particular person or contact.
