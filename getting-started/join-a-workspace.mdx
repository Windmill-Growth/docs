---
title: "Join a workspace"
description: "Accept an invitation and start working with your team in traxy."
---

Join the workspace your team already uses so you can work with its shared leads and configuration.

1. Open the invitation email and follow its link.
2. Sign in or create your account using the invited email address.
3. Accept the invitation.
4. Check the workspace name before opening leads or changing settings.

Joining an existing workspace does not require you to create another ICP or complete new-workspace checkout. Your role and the workspace's access determine which actions are available.

## Start working with your team

Open **Leads** to review qualified leads and shared views. Use **trax** for a new request. Review [members and permissions](/workspace/team) before changing shared configuration.

If the invitation has expired or was revoked, ask an admin for a new invitation. If you already belong to several workspaces, use the workspace switcher to select the intended one.
