A clean traxy account starts with knowing who not to track and ends with knowing exactly where your credits are going. This walkthrough covers blocklisting domains you don't want signals from, fine-tuning enrichment so you're not wasting credits on low-fit leads, and understanding how credit usage and plans work.

## Understand credits & plans

Not every lead deserves your attention — or your credits. Here's how to keep both in check:

- Blocklist domains you don't want signals from, so they never show up as leads in the first place
- Snooze a lead temporarily or mark it as a client to keep it out of your active pipeline
- Use advanced filters and auto-enrich by email, phone, or both, so enrichment matches how you'll actually reach out
- Set a minimum ICP score before a lead gets enriched, so credits go to your best-fit leads first
- Credit usage counts up as you qualify leads — one qualification uses one credit — and you can upgrade across tiers from 15K to 100K as you grow

Clean inputs, smart spend — that's how you get the most out of every credit.
