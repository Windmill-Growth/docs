This is a walkthrough of the core traxy workflow, start to finish:

- **Dashboard** — a snapshot of your account's activity and how leads match your ICP.
- **ICP & buying signals** — how to define who you're targeting and what signals count as intent.
- **Agent & watchlist** — how the Agent runs against your budget, and how to track specific profiles and posts.
- **Leads** — where qualified leads land, ready to review and act on.
- **Integrations** — connecting traxy to Slack, MCP, and other outbound tools your team uses.

By the end, you'll have a clear picture of how traxy moves a lead from signal to outreach and where to go next to fine-tune any part of that flow for your own team.
