---
title: "Use signals in outreach"
description: "Choose a message angle that matches the evidence."
---

Read the lead's source and date before choosing an angle.

| Evidence | A useful approach | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A relevant comment or question | Respond to the specific problem raised. | Claiming an intent to purchase. |
| A relevant post by the person | Connect your offer to the topic they discussed. | Inventing a current project or budget. |
| Engagement with someone else's post | Use the topic as context when the interaction supports it. | Claiming they endorse every idea in the post. |
| Company or role fit without a strong signal | Use a factual role-based introduction. | Inventing an interaction for personalization. |

Signal labels and ICP match are separate. Review [Signals](/discovery/signals), then test a small campaign sample before approving outreach.
