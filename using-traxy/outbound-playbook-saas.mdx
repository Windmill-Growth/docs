---
title: "SaaS outreach"
description: "Build a campaign around a defined software buyer and relevant evidence."
---

Define the roles and company characteristics that match your software. Choose signals tied to the problem it solves, then inspect the resulting qualified leads.

Save an audience view and ask trax to research the facts needed for your offer. In the campaign test, check whether each company-specific claim has support. Remove claims about tooling, budget, or urgency that the research does not establish.

Review the sender, message steps, and delivery mode before launch. Use tags and assignments to coordinate follow-up across members.

Start with [Lead criteria](/discovery/lead-criteria) and [Review and launch](/campaigns/review-and-launch).
