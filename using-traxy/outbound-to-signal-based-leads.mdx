---
title: "Outbound from qualified signals"
description: "Build outreach from verified lead context."
---

Start with a qualified audience and an observation you can support from the lead's recorded evidence.

1. Review **Lead criteria** and **Signals**.
2. Filter leads by fit and relevant signal context.
3. Save the audience as a shared view.
4. Ask the Agent to prepare a campaign using that view and your offer.
5. Test the plan and inspect the facts and messages.
6. Review the launch dialog and delivery mode before approving.
7. Handle responses in Inbox or the chosen destination and record meeting outcomes.

Reference only facts the source supports. Do not infer a person's budget, intent, or dissatisfaction from a generic interaction. If the evidence is too weak, change the message or skip the lead.

Use [Review and launch](/campaigns/review-and-launch) for the product steps and [Signals](/discovery/signals) for signal labels.
