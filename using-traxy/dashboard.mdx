---

Use Dashboard for a current summary of your workspace.

## Review lead volume

The top cards show:

- **Total leads**, including the number added in the last 30 days
- **Avg leads per day**
- **Minimum ICP match**

The minimum ICP match controls which people qualify for your workspace. Raise it for stricter matching. Lower it to include more borderline matches.

Owners and admins can change the threshold. The control applies the new value when you commit the change.

## Read the leads-per-day chart

Use the chart to compare lead discovery and credit use over time.

Choose **7 Days**, **1 Month**, **3 Month**, or **All time**. The chart can separate:

- Agent leads
- Watchlist leads
- other lead sources
- credits used
- average ICP match

Use the same time range when you compare changes after an ICP or threshold update.

## Review live leads

**Live leads** shows the newest qualified leads first. Open a person to review the lead detail, or click **View all** to continue in **Leads**.

## Understand your audience

The audience section groups qualified leads by:

- title
- company size
- location
- industry

Use the strongest segment to see which part of your ICP is producing the most lead activity. These charts are aggregate summaries, not a replacement for the lead list.

## Review top performance

The top-performing section compares recent results over the last seven days.

- **Watchlist** highlights profiles producing useful lead signal.
- **Posts** highlights posts producing qualified leads.

Open the related profile or post when you want the underlying details.

<Tip>
  If lead volume is low, compare the Agent state, Watchlist activity, ICP, and minimum match before changing several settings at once.
</Tip>
