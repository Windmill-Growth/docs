title: "Watchlist"
description: "Monitor LinkedIn profiles and qualify people who engage with their posts."
---

Use Watchlist to monitor people whose LinkedIn audiences matter to your workspace.

## Choose a relationship

Watchlist supports:

- **Competitor**
- **Teammate**
- **Influencer**
- **Investor**
- **Custom**

Use **Custom** for a reusable relationship such as client, partner, or customer.

## Add profiles

1. Open **Watchlist**.
2. Click **Add LinkedIn Profile**.
3. Paste a LinkedIn profile URL.
4. Verify the profile.
5. Confirm the relationship and company.
6. Add the profile.

Use batch mode to paste up to 20 LinkedIn profile URLs at once. The 20-URL bound applies to one verification request, not to the size of your Watchlist.

Watchlist profiles are unlimited. A separate limit can apply when a relationship requires a competitor company.

## Understand the first discovery

A profile's first successful addition creates one free initial discovery.

traxy freezes up to five recent posts and examines them until it finds five new qualified leads or reaches the end of the frozen window.

The discovery belongs to the profile:

- adding a duplicate does not create another discovery
- restoring a removed profile does not create another discovery
- pausing or removing the profile suspends it
- resuming or restoring the profile continues the same discovery

Qualification after this one-time discovery uses the workspace credit balance.

## Understand ongoing qualification

After you add a profile, traxy manages the five most recent posts published from that point forward.

This is a sliding trailing-five window. It is always on for active Watchlist monitoring and cannot be disabled or reduced. Already examined posts process only new, deduplicated engagement.

Use manual **Find Leads** in [Post Signals](/using-traxy/content-calendar) for a post outside the managed window.

## Use tabs and summary cards

Use **All Profiles**, **Competitors**, **Teammates**, **Influencers**, **Investors**, and **Custom** to narrow the table.

The summary cards show:

- **All Profiles Monitored**
- **Qualified Posts**
- **Engagements**
- **Qualified leads**

Use **Monitor** and **Archived** to move between active and removed profiles.

## Review the table

Each row can show the profile, relationship, company, qualified posts, qualified leads, **CPL**, state, and date added.

**CPL** means credits used per qualified lead in the current billing window.

Open a profile to review:

- audience ICP match
- qualified posts and leads
- credits used in the current billing window
- recent top leads
- tracked posts and qualification status

You can pause, resume, remove, or restore a manually added profile. Some teammate profiles can be created from workspace member context and may have different controls.

## Use recommendations

**Recommended for you** can suggest profiles based on your workspace context. Review the relationship and expected signal before you add one. Dismiss a recommendation when it is not useful.

<Info>
  High-engagement profiles can produce more qualification work. Review **Post Signals** and credit usage when you want to understand the cost of a profile's activity.
</Info>
