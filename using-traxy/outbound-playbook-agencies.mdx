---
title: "Agency outreach"
description: "Keep each service audience and campaign focused on a clear offer."
---

Choose one service and buyer segment for a campaign. Describe the company context, target roles, and signals that make the service relevant.

Save a shared view for the audience. Ask trax to prepare a campaign that uses a supported observation and a concrete next step. Review sample results before approving launch.

Use assignments and shared notes so members can see who is handling each lead. Keep separate client or business contexts in the appropriate workspace and verify the workspace before making changes.

Follow [Members and workspaces](/workspace/team) and [Create a campaign](/campaigns/create) for the product steps.
