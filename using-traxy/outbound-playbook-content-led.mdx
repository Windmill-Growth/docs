---
title: "Content-led outreach"
description: "Review people engaging with relevant content and prepare a campaign."
---

Track your relevant profiles or add a specific post in **Tracking**. Inspect the qualified leads and the actual interaction behind each result.

Save a view for the audience you want to work with. Ask the Agent to draft a campaign that responds to the topic and offers a useful next step. Review the test for unsupported assumptions before launch.

A reaction alone does not prove that a person read the whole post or wants to buy. Keep the wording proportional to the evidence.

After launch, review replies and meetings rather than treating engagement volume as a sales outcome. Follow [Create a campaign](/campaigns/create) for setup.
