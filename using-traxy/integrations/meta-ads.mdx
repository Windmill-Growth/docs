---
title: "Meta Ads"
description: "Export qualified leads from traxy, upload them as a Meta customer list, and build a lookalike audience."
---

Use the Meta Ads integration when you want to turn reviewed qualified leads into a Meta Custom Audience and a lookalike audience.

The best seed list is specific, clean, and close to your ICP. Include both email and phone number when traxy has them so Meta has more identifiers to match.

## Before you start

Make sure you have:

- access to the right Meta business account and ad account
- permission to create audiences in Meta Ads
- the Meta Ads integration available in **Integrations**
- a reviewed list of qualified leads you are allowed to use for advertising
- email and phone enrichment where possible

<Info>
  Customer lists contain personal identifiers. Only export and upload contacts you have permission to use for advertising, and follow Meta's customer list terms.
</Info>

## Export the audience from traxy

1. Open **Integrations**.
2. Open **Meta Ads**.
3. Start an audience export.
4. Choose the qualified lead segment you want to use.
5. Prefer leads with both **Email** and **Phone** values.
6. If needed, enrich selected leads before you export.
7. Keep **Email** and **Phone** selected as identifiers.
8. Export the list.

Upload the exact file traxy gives you. Do not rename columns, split email and phone into separate files, or reformat phone numbers unless your team has a required cleanup step.

If you need a tighter seed list, start from **Leads** and filter by ICP match, intent, monitored profile, source, or owner before you export.

## Upload the list in Meta Ads

1. Go to your Meta business account.
2. Open **Audiences**.
3. Click **Create audience**.
4. Choose **Custom audience**.
5. Choose **Customer list**.
6. Upload the file you exported from traxy.
7. Accept Meta's customer list terms if Meta prompts you.
8. Map the traxy columns to Meta identifiers.
9. Use **Email** for email addresses and **Phone** for phone numbers.
10. Name the audience clearly, such as `traxy - qualified leads - Q2 2026`.
11. Finish the import and wait for Meta to process the audience.

Meta may take time to match the uploaded identifiers. Wait until the audience is ready before you use it in an ad set or as a lookalike source.

## Create a lookalike audience

1. In **Audiences**, click **Create audience**.
2. Choose **Lookalike audience**.
3. Select the traxy customer list Custom Audience as the source.
4. Choose the countries or regions you want to reach.
5. Start with a close lookalike size when available.
6. Create broader lookalike variants separately when you want to test scale.
7. Name the audience clearly, such as `traxy - qualified leads - 1% lookalike - US`.
8. Create the audience and wait for Meta to finish building it.

If Meta cannot create the lookalike, the source audience may be too small or still processing. Export a larger qualified list, broaden your traxy filters, or wait for the customer list audience to finish matching.

## Track both audiences

Track the original customer list and the lookalike audience separately so you can see which one is helping.

Use the customer list when you want to:

- retarget known qualified leads
- exclude known leads from prospecting campaigns
- compare known-lead performance against new-audience performance

Use the lookalike audience when you want to:

- find new people similar to your qualified leads
- test prospecting from a seed list that already reflects your ICP
- scale beyond the people traxy already found

For cleaner reporting, put the customer list and lookalike audience in separate ad sets when you test them. Use clear campaign names, ad set names, and URL tracking so you can compare spend, cost per lead, conversion rate, and downstream lead quality.

## Refresh the audience

Refresh the Meta customer list when your qualified lead pool changes meaningfully.

You can update the same customer list when you want Meta campaigns and related lookalikes to keep using the latest source. Create a new dated audience when you want to compare one export against another.

If match quality looks weak, check whether the export includes both email and phone, whether phone numbers include country context, and whether you uploaded the unedited traxy file.
