Use traxy to find people who match your ICP, understand the signals behind each qualified lead, and turn that context into outreach.

Start with your company and targeting. Then ask the Agent to find leads or posts, review an audience, or help you build a campaign. You review proposed actions before they run.

## Choose where to work

| Area | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| **Agent** | Work through requests in a chat and review results beside it. |
| **Leads** | Review qualified leads, save audiences, and collaborate with members. |
| **Campaigns** | Inspect campaign progress, destinations, and leads awaiting approval. |
| **Inbox** | Read conversations and reply to leads. |
| **Performance** and **Meetings** | Review discovery results and attributed meetings. |
| **trax control panel** | Adjust lead criteria, signals, tracking, and discovery runs. |
| **Settings** | Manage your workspace, sender accounts, plan, and billing. |

Connect other tools through [Integrations](/integrations/overview). For application development, use the [public API and MCP reference](/api-reference/overview).
