Use traxy to find people who match your ICP, understand the signals behind each qualified lead, and turn that context into outreach.

Start with your company and targeting. Then ask trax to find leads or posts, review an audience, or help you build a campaign. You review proposed actions before they run.

## Choose where to work

| Area | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| **trax** | Start a chat, return to earlier conversations, and inspect discovery runs. |
| **Leads** | Review qualified leads and open built-in, shared, or personal views. |
| **Targeting** | Adjust lead criteria, signals, and tracked people, posts, or companies. |
| **Outbound** | Work with Inbox, Campaigns, and Meetings. |
| **Insights** | Review discovery results and audience patterns. |
| **Settings** | Manage your workspace, integrations, sender accounts, plan, and billing. |
