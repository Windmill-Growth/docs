title: "Lead Searcher"
description: "Search for matching LinkedIn profiles by ICP, filters, and recent engagement in traxy."
---

Use Lead Searcher when you want to find profiles that match a specific ICP or buyer description.

Lead Searcher uses vector search. It ranks profiles by semantic match and shows company, location, recent warm signal, follower count, and links you can open.

## Search by ICP

1. Open **Lead Searcher**.
2. Describe the audience in **ICP**.
3. Set the **Limit** for how many matches you want.
4. Add filters if you need a tighter search.
5. Click **Search**.

Good ICP search text is specific but short.

For example:

```text
VP Sales at B2B SaaS companies in the US with recent LinkedIn engagement
```

## Use filters

Use filters when the ICP text alone is too broad.

Available filters include:

- **Country codes**
- **Titles**
- **Industries**
- **Company sizes**
- **Locations**

Enter comma-separated values such as `US, CA`, `VP Sales, Founder`, or `11-50, 51-200`.

## Review matches

The matches table shows:

- rank
- lead name and semantic match score
- title
- company and company details
- location
- latest warm signal
- engagement counts
- followers
- LinkedIn and post links when available

Use **Open** to review the LinkedIn profile. Use the warm signal action to open the latest engagement source when traxy has one.

## When to use Lead Searcher

Use Lead Searcher when you want to:

- explore a new ICP
- find more people similar to your best leads
- research a segment before changing your main ICP
- inspect recent engagement before deciding whether to monitor a profile or qualify a post

Use [Agent](/using-traxy/agent) when you want traxy to scan matching profiles continuously. Use [Post Signals](/using-traxy/content-calendar) when you want to find leads from a specific LinkedIn post.
